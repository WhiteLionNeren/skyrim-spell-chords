The source of Spell Chords is public domain (Unlicense). SpellChords.dll statically links CommonLibSSE-NG v8.3.0
(https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibVR, commit abe9ca7), which is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later with the exceptions below, so
the DLL as distributed is covered by GPL-3.0-or-later (full text: COPYING.txt in this folder). Its complete corresponding
source is the repository above together with its CommonLibSSE-NG submodule. Also compiled in through CommonLibSSE-NG:
spdlog, fmt (MIT) and rapidcsv (BSD-3-Clause); their licenses are in THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt in this folder.
